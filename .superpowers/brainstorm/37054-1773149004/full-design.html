Design Principles

Minimal Sharp
No borders or hard shadows. Depth through background contrast only. Cards are subtle surface-colored containers.
Expressive Animations
Spring physics for interactive elements. Staggered list entrances. Smooth 300ms page transitions. Numbers animate up on load.
Tight Typography
Negative letter-spacing on headings (-0.5px). Heavy weights (700-800) for numbers and titles. Light weights for labels.
Color Restraint
Cyan only for primary CTAs and active states. Semantic colors (green/yellow/red) for status. Most UI is monochrome with subtle tints.
