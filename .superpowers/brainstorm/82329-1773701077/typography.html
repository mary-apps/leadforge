A. System Font (SF Pro)

Keep the current system font. It's readable, familiar, and renders perfectly on iOS. Zero loading overhead. The editorial feel comes from spacing and weight contrast, not the typeface itself.

B. Inter

Popular in modern SaaS (Linear, Vercel, Resend). Tighter metrics than SF Pro, excellent readability at small sizes. Gives a slightly more "designed" feel while staying neutral. Adds ~100KB bundle.

C. DM Sans + System

DM Sans for headings and emphasis (warm geometric shapes), system font for body text. Two-font pairing creates subtle personality without sacrificing iOS native feel in running text.
