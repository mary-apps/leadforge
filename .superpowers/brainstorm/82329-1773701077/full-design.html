Design Principles

Typography-driven hierarchy
DM Sans headings (700-800 weight) create structure. System font body text stays readable. Size and weight do the work — no borders, no backgrounds, no icons needed.
Color only for meaning
Green = good, amber = warning, red = poor. Everything else is grayscale. The accent is near-black — buttons, links, and active states blend into the content naturally.
Dividers over containers
No card borders. Single-pixel dividers separate items. Content floats on the white canvas. Spacing and alignment create groups, not boxes.
Generous whitespace
Content breathes. Sections are separated by vertical space, not visual chrome. The empty space is part of the design.
